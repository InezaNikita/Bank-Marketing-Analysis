# Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis – Questions & Answers

1. Was the marketing campaign successful?
Yes. The campaign achieved a 47.4% subscription rate, with about 5.87K customers subscribing to the deposit offer out of 11K total customers.

2. Which age group subscribed the most?
Customers between 30–49 years old had the highest number of subscriptions, with nearly 3K subscriptions recorded.

3. Did previous campaign outcomes affect subscription success?
Yes. Customers with previous successful campaign outcomes showed higher subscription results compared to customers with failed or other campaign outcomes.

4. Which marital group subscribed the most?
Married customers represented the largest share of subscriptions at about 52%, followed by single customers at around 36%.

5. What does the subscription distribution show?
The subscription distribution shows that 52.6% of customers subscribed to the deposit offer, while 47.3% did not subscribe.
